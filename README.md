# Linear Algebra — CUSAT M.Tech AI & Software Engineering

A professionally designed **LaTeX Beamer presentation** covering the Linear Algebra topics prescribed for the **M.Tech Computer Science & Engineering — Artificial Intelligence & Software Engineering** programme at Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT).

The presentation is designed for **M.Tech-level learning, exam preparation, and academic reference**, with emphasis on mathematical intuition, formulas, geometric interpretation, numerical examples, and connections to Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
